How to get your holiday link

  1. Go to TUI, Jet2holidays, easyJet holidays or Crystal Ski and search with your airport, dates and party.
  2. Tap the hotel you want so you can see its total price.
  3. Copy the link. On a phone, tap the address bar or the share button and choose Copy.
  4. Paste it in the box below.
Your hotel, dates and party are picked up from the link automatically.

Prices are checked once a day on the holiday company's own website and are for guidance only. Final prices are confirmed when you book. Your details are used to run your price watch and, if you tick the second box, to send you offers. See the privacy notice. Every alert email has a link to stop watching.

Price watch FAQs

Which holiday companies can you watch?

TUI, Jet2holidays, easyJet holidays and Crystal Ski for now. I sell lots more than these, so if your holiday is with someone else, WhatsApp me and I'll help.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to book with me. If you do, you get the same holiday and the same ABTA protection, plus me looking after you from booking to landing home.

How often will you email me?

Only when the total price changes. If it stays the same, you won't hear a thing. Small wobbles under £20 are ignored so you're not bombarded.

How do I stop it?

Every email has a "stop watching" link. Watches also end automatically on your departure date.
