SOUTH AFRICA

Table Mountain, Cape Point's penguins, the Winelands and a genuine Kruger safari, three real TUI tours compared, weather by month and what it actually costs.

What Jake says about South Africa

South Africa is a different kind of trip to the rest of my destination guides, and I think that is exactly why clients love it. TUI is the only one of my three operators, TUI, Jet2holidays and easyJet holidays, that currently sells it, and it does not sell it as a week in one hotel with a pool. It sells it as an escorted tour, flights, transfers, a local English speaking guide and a string of real named lodges and hotels all bundled together, so you are not left trying to stitch together Cape Town, the Winelands and a safari yourself.

That makes it one of the easiest genuinely life list trips I book. You can go purely for the wildlife on a lodge based safari around the Entabeni Wildlife Conservancy, or go big on the full Highlights of South Africa tour and tick off Kruger, the Garden Route and Cape Town in one booking. I will come back to all three real, bookable options further down.

Jake's top tip

The first question I ask is whether this is a safari trip with Cape Town as a bonus, or a Cape Town trip with safari as a bonus. It decides which of the three TUI tours I point a client towards.

Trip length & who it suits

TUI's two lodge based safari tours run 8 nights, 9 days, built entirely around game drives from a single base. The full Highlights of South Africa tour runs 14 nights, 16 days, and covers Johannesburg, Kruger, KwaZulu Natal, the Garden Route and Cape Town in one trip. All three suit couples, honeymooners and small groups after a proper once in a lifetime holiday rather than a poolside week, and all three have a minimum tour age of 12, so they are not aimed at young families. Anyone who wants the shortest possible flight or a straightforward beach stay with no organised itinerary is better suited to one of my other destinations.

Getting there

TUI's tours fly indirectly into OR Tambo International Airport in Johannesburg from a choice of UK airports including Heathrow, Gatwick, Manchester, Glasgow, Edinburgh and Birmingham, with TUI's own travel information quoting a flight time of around 11 hours 30 minutes. If a client wants Cape Town specifically, outside of a TUI tour, both British Airways and Virgin Atlantic fly nonstop from Heathrow to Cape Town, taking around 11 hours 40 minutes.

Jake's top tip

All of a TUI tour's internal flights and transfers, including the one between Johannesburg and Cape Town on the longer tour, are included in the price and arranged for you. You only have to book the one trip.

Weather by month

Cape Town runs on a Mediterranean style climate that is the reverse of the UK's, warm and dry in summer, cooler and wetter in winter.

Kruger and the rest of the safari country run on the opposite pattern to Cape Town. May to September is the dry season and the best stretch for game viewing, since thinner vegetation and animals gathering at waterholes make wildlife much easier to spot, while October to April is warmer and greener with more frequent afternoon storms.

!
Jake's top tip

Cape Town's rain and Kruger's dry season line up in the same months, June to August, which is exactly why the longer Highlights of South Africa tour works so well then. You get Kruger at its wildlife viewing best and simply dress for cooler, showery days once you reach Cape Town at the end of the trip.

Best time to visit

June to September is the standout window if safari is the priority, dry and cool inland for the best game viewing, even though Cape Town itself is at its wettest then. November to March is the better call if Cape Town's beaches and warm weather matter most, hot and dry on the coast, though this does overlap with the wetter, greener season in safari country. April, May and October are solid shoulder months either way, with fewer crowds and a reasonable balance of weather in both halves of the country.

Where to stay

This is not a one hotel holiday, so where you stay depends entirely on which of the three TUI tours you book. The two lodge based safari tours keep you in a single lake view lodge at the Entabeni Wildlife Conservancy in Limpopo Province for the whole trip, choosing between a 4 star or 5 star standard. The full Highlights of South Africa tour moves through 11 different hotels of a 3 and 4 star standard as it crosses the country, from Johannesburg and Hazyview near Kruger, through KwaZulu Natal and the Garden Route, to a final two nights in Cape Town itself.

!
Jake's top tip

Facilities at tour hotels can vary and TUI does not guarantee things like spa, gym or pool access at every stop, since the point of a tour is the itinerary rather than any one hotel. I set that expectation with clients upfront.

Getting around

Every TUI tour includes return airport transfers plus all transport during the trip itself, whether that is a safari minibus, a 4x4 game viewing vehicle or a coach between stops, all with a local English speaking guide. You do not need to arrange a single transfer or self drive leg yourself. South Africa drives on the left, the same as the UK, which is worth knowing if a client wants to extend a trip independently with their own car hire.

Places to visit

A few of the sights that make it onto the Highlights of South Africa tour, or that I'd suggest adding if a client extends their time in Cape Town.

Table Mountain

Cape Town's flat topped landmark, reachable by a rotating cable car with views across the city, Table Bay and all the way to Robben Island on a clear day. Part of the city tour on the Highlights of South Africa tour.

Boulders Beach & Cape Point

A colony of African penguins nesting right on the sand at Boulders Beach, usually paired with a trip out to Cape Point and the Cape of Good Hope at the peninsula's tip. One of the most popular half day trips from Cape Town.

The Cape Winelands

Stellenbosch and Franschhoek sit less than an hour from Cape Town, with vineyard after vineyard set against mountain backdrops. The Highlights of South Africa tour includes a Western Cape wine tasting on its way into the city.

The Garden Route & Knysna

One of the world's most scenic drives, running along the southern coast through forests, lagoons and seaside towns including Knysna, where the Highlights of South Africa tour builds in a free day. Starts from Cape Town for anyone extending a trip independently.

Quick practical info

The essentials, at a glance.

CurrencySouth African Rand (ZAR)
Plug typeType M, and increasingly the newer Type N, different from the UK's Type G, so you will need a travel adaptor. Mains supply is 230V
LanguageEnglish is one of 11 official languages and is widely used in hotels and tourist areas, alongside Zulu, Xhosa and Afrikaans among others
Flight time from the UKAround 11 hours 30 minutes from Gatwick to Johannesburg on a TUI tour. British Airways and Virgin Atlantic both fly nonstop from Heathrow to Cape Town in around 11 hours 40 minutes
Time differenceSouth Africa does not change its clocks, so it is 2 hours ahead of the UK in winter and 1 hour ahead during British Summer Time
Entry requirementsUK passport holders can visit visa free for up to 90 days. Your passport needs to be valid for at least 30 days beyond your planned departure from South Africa with 2 blank pages. From 1 July 2026, all travellers entering or leaving South Africa must also complete a mandatory online traveller declaration form between 24 and 48 hours before travel
DrivingLeft hand side, the same as the UK
