KENYA

Diani and Bamburi compared, weather by month, where to stay, things to do, three ways to add a safari and what it actually costs, everything you need to plan a trip to Kenya's coast.

What Jake says about Kenya

Kenya is the one I reach for when a client wants a proper beach holiday that can also turn into something much bigger. Diani on the south coast has some of the best white sand I've seen anywhere, warm turquoise water and a laid back, barefoot feel, and it sits within easy reach of a genuine Big Five safari if you want to build one in. TUI is the operator I use for Kenya, packaging flights and hotel together, and it is the only one of the three operators I sell, TUI, Jet2holidays and easyJet holidays, that currently flies this far.

What makes Kenya stand out from Zanzibar or the Caribbean for me is how easy TUI has made it to turn a beach week into a proper safari and beach trip without having to book anything separately. There are several real, named, priced itineraries you can book directly through TUI that combine time in the bush with time on the sand, which I'll come back to further down.

!
Jake's top tip

I always ask a client upfront whether a safari is even a maybe for this trip. It changes how many nights I'd suggest booking and which coastal hotel I'd point them towards, since some combine much more neatly with a safari add-on than others.

Trip length & who it suits

Seven to ten nights is the sweet spot for a pure beach stay on the coast, long enough to make the long flight worthwhile without feeling like too much time in one place. If you want to add a safari, I'd budget ten to fourteen nights in total so neither half of the trip feels rushed, nine nights is the shortest I've seen a genuine combined safari and beach package run to. It suits couples and honeymooners who want Diani's quieter, more boutique feel, families after a big all inclusive resort with a kids' club on the north coast around Bamburi, and anyone who likes the idea of elephants and lions one week and a beach sunbed the next. It is less suited to anyone who needs the shortest possible flight today, since this is a genuine long haul destination, or anyone expecting every hotel to feel as polished top to bottom as somewhere like the Canaries, parts of the Kenyan coast still feel refreshingly unpolished.

Getting there

TUI's coastal package holidays fly into Moi International Airport (MBA) at Mombasa, and TUI's own travel information quotes a flight time of around 9 hours from Gatwick. If a safari built around Nairobi is more your focus, it's worth knowing that Kenya Airways launched the first ever nonstop service between London Gatwick and Nairobi in July 2025, flying three times a week, where previously every scheduled option routed via a connection. Scheduled carriers flying into Mombasa itself, such as British Airways, KLM and Ethiopian Airlines, still route via a connection such as Nairobi, Amsterdam or Addis Ababa rather than flying nonstop to the coast.

!
Jake's top tip

For a straightforward coastal beach holiday I book the TUI package as normal, flights and hotel together with one flight time to plan around. If a client is building their own Nairobi-based safari independently of a coastal stay, I flag the new direct Kenya Airways route, since it's a genuinely new option that didn't exist a couple of years ago.

Jake's top tip

The April and May rains put a lot of clients off, but they're also when prices are at their lowest. Showers tend to come as short, heavy afternoon bursts rather than ruining a whole day, so I don't rule the long rains out automatically, it depends what the client wants from the trip.

Best time to visit

January to March is hot and dry and a safe bet if your dates are fixed around that window. June to October is drier and a touch cooler on the coast, and it also overlaps with the best inland safari viewing, making it the natural pick if you're planning a combined trip, though August is busy with school holidays. April and May are the long rains, by far the quietest and cheapest time to travel, with the trade off being more frequent afternoon showers. November is a good value shoulder month with the short rains usually limited to brief showers, and December is hot and dry again but busy and pricier over Christmas and New Year.

Where to stay

Kenya's package hotels cluster in two main areas, Diani on the south coast and Bamburi on the north coast, each with a distinct feel.

Diani (the south coast)

A long, white sand beach with the widest choice of TUI hotels, a lively strip of beachfront restaurants and bars, and the easiest access to boat trips out to Wasini Island. This is the area I'd point couples, honeymooners and anyone after Kenya's most sociable coastal base towards.

Bamburi (the north coast)

Closer to Mombasa town and the airport, with some of Kenya's biggest all inclusive family resorts and easy access to Mombasa's Old Town and Fort Jesus. This is the area I'd steer larger families and groups towards for the shortest transfer and the most resort facilities on site.

!
Jake's top tip

Diani has the edge for a honeymoon or a couple's trip, Bamburi has the edge for a big family group who want the shortest possible transfer and the most pools and kids' clubs on site. I ask which matters more before I recommend either.

Getting around

Airport transfers are included as part of the TUI package. Kenya drives on the left, the same as the UK, but almost every client sticks to transfers, taxis and organised day trips rather than self drive. A private driver for the day is easy to arrange through your hotel for a trip into Mombasa, a Wasini Island boat day or a safari day trip, and is by far the most relaxed way to see more of the coast beyond the resort.

Recommended hotels

Four real, bookable picks across Diani and Bamburi. TUI is the operator I use for Kenya, so all four of these are booked through TUI.

Best value

Southern Palms Beach Resort, Diani

A 4 star beach resort directly on Diani's sand, with two pools, a ten acre tropical garden, four restaurants and five bars. A straightforward, good value base for anyone who wants a solid resort without paying for a five star price tag. Bookable through TUI.

Best for families

Baobab Beach Resort & Spa, Diani

Set across 80 acres of gardens with 500 metres of beach, family suites for two adults and two children, a kids' scuba club and half, full or all inclusive board options. Winner of Africa's Leading Family Resort, and a hotel I know well since it's the one TUI itself uses on its own combined safari and beach packages. Bookable through TUI.

Best for couples

Leopard Beach Resort & Spa, Diani

A smart casual, "Chui Class" resort with honeymoon suites, sixteen private pool villas known as The Residences, a spa and four restaurants including a Chaine des Rotisseurs dining room. The one I'd point towards for a couple wanting Diani's most polished address. Bookable through TUI.

Best for big groups

Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort & Spa, Bamburi

One of the coast's biggest resorts, with around 300 rooms across five categories, five swimming pools, a hot tub, tennis and family rooms. The shortest transfer from Mombasa airport of any hotel in this guide, and the one I'd book for a large family group or multi-generation trip. Bookable through TUI.

Hotel availability, board basis and pricing change regularly, always confirm the live details with Jake before booking.

Places to visit

A few of the sights worth building into an itinerary beyond the hotel pool.

Wasini Island & Kisite Marine Park

A dhow sailing trip from Diani to Wasini Island, with a good chance of spotting dolphins and some of Kenya's best snorkelling in the Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park. One of the most popular full day trips from the south coast.

Mombasa Old Town & Fort Jesus

A UNESCO World Heritage 16th century Portuguese fort, winding old town streets, the famous Mombasa Tusks and a lively spice market. Usually booked as a half or full day trip from either coastal area.

Shimba Hills National Reserve

A forested reserve close to Diani, home to the rare sable antelope alongside elephants, giraffes and buffalo. A good, easy introduction to safari for anyone short on time who still wants a taste of the bush close to the coast.

Tsavo East National Park

One of the world's largest national parks, known for its "red elephants", dust-stained from the park's red soil, plus lions and the Galana River. Bookable as a long day trip or an overnight stay from either coastal area.

Combining Kenya's coast with a safari

Kenya is one of the easiest places I sell to turn a beach week into a proper safari trip, and TUI sells several real, bookable itineraries that do exactly that. These aren't a generic add-on, each one is a named, priced tour you can book directly through TUI. Here are three genuinely different options.

The Safari Taster, 9 nights

Five nights of all inclusive safari, staying at Ashnil Aruba Lodge in Tsavo East, Salt Lick Safari Lodge in the Taita Hills and Amboseli Serena Lodge, with views of Mount Kilimanjaro, then four nights unwinding at Baobab Beach Resort on Diani Beach. Flights, transfers and 23kg luggage included. From £2,742 per person.

A Taste of Kenya Safari, 11 days

An 11 day beach and safari holiday built around six game drives, with an overnight stay at Salt Lick Safari Lodge in the Taita Hills before finishing on the coast. TUI's own named package. From £2,783 per person.

Highlights of Kenya, 12 days

A 12 day tour from Nairobi to Diani Beach, with most meals and eight included "experiences" plus one night at a stilted safari lodge with views of Mount Kilimanjaro. Flights available from Heathrow, Gatwick or Manchester. From £2,624 per person.

Prices shown are TUI's own "from" prices for two adults sharing at time of writing and change regularly with dates and availability. All three are flight-inclusive, ATOL protected tours booked through TUI, separate from the main Kenya coast beach package above.

!
Jake's top tip

A safari add-on is the single biggest upgrade I suggest to Kenya clients who have the extra days and budget. The Safari Taster is the one I point most clients towards since it's the shortest of the three and still covers three proper safari lodges, while Highlights of Kenya suits anyone who wants a slightly longer, more structured tour with more meals and experiences built in.
