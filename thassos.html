THASSOS

Greece's greenest island, a Greek island with no airport of its own, and a genuinely different pace to the Greek islands most people already know. Here's exactly how I book Thassos, the hotels I can price up for you, and what it actually costs.

What Jake says about Thassos

Thassos sits in the far north of the Aegean, closer to Thessaloniki than to the islands clients usually ask me about, and it shows in a good way. It's covered in pine forest and marble mountains rather than the bare, scrubby hills you get further south, the beaches are genuinely some of the best in Greece, and it still gets a fraction of the visitor numbers of Corfu, Crete or Zante. There's no airport on the island itself, which keeps it quieter and is exactly why most of my clients haven't been before.

I describe Thassos to clients as the Greek island for people who think they have already done the Greek islands. It suits anyone who wants real beaches and a proper local feel without a single big resort strip, rather than clients who want to roll straight off the plane into their hotel.

Trip length & who it suits

Because of the extra ferry leg to reach the island, seven nights is the shortest length I'd suggest, so the travelling time doesn't eat into the holiday too much. Ten to fourteen nights is the sweet spot, giving you time to properly explore the coast road, fit in a hike up Mount Ipsarion or through the old villages, and still spend plenty of days doing nothing but swimming. Thassos suits couples and families after a quieter, greener Greek island, hikers and nature lovers thanks to the marble mountains and forest trails running right down to the coast, and clients who want real, working tavernas rather than a resort strip built purely for tourists.

It's less suited to clients who want nightlife or a big choice of bars and clubs, Thassos is a relaxed, family friendly island rather than a party destination, and it's also not the pick for anyone who wants to be in their hotel within an hour of landing, since the ferry crossing is part of every trip here.

Getting there

Thassos has no airport of its own, so every trip here involves a short ferry crossing from the Greek mainland. Right now the most straightforward route is to fly into Thessaloniki, the region's main international airport, then transfer by road to the port at Keramoti for the crossing. From summer 2027, new seasonal flights are launching direct to the much closer Kavala International Airport, around a 15 minute drive from Keramoti, opening up a genuinely quick route onto the island from several UK airports including Birmingham, Manchester and London Stansted for the first time. The ferry itself from Keramoti to Limenas, the island's main town, takes about 40 minutes and runs dozens of times a day in summer, or you can cross from Kavala itself to Skala Prinos in around 1 hour 15 minutes. Best time to visit

June to September is the main window for a beach focused trip, with July and August the hottest, driest and busiest months on the island. September is a genuine sweet spot, the sea is at its warmest all year and the crowds have thinned right out. May and October work well for clients more interested in hiking the marble mountains and exploring the old villages than lying on a beach all day, the weather is mild and pleasant even if the sea is a touch cool for a long swim. November through March is Thassos's quiet season, many hotels close completely and it's really only worth considering for a short, low key visit rather than a typical holiday.

!
Jake's top tip

If a client asks me for the single best value window on Thassos, I always say September. Same warm sea as August, a fraction of the crowds, and noticeably lower prices.

Where to stay

Four real, bookable picks across the island.

Best value

A for Art Hotel

4 star boutique hotel in Limenas, the island's capital, set inside a restored early 20th century tobacco warehouse right by the old port and the ancient Agora. An outdoor pool, restaurant and bar, with design led rooms that mix the original building with contemporary art. A properly characterful base with everything on foot. I can price this one up for you.

Best for a quiet cove

Makryammos Bungalows

4 star bungalow style hotel set in pine forest a short drive from Limenas, built around its own small sandy cove. Outdoor and children's pools, tennis courts and a handful of restaurants, with rooms spread through the pine trees rather than stacked in a single block. A lovely pick for families who want their own patch of beach. I can price this one up for you.

Best big beachfront resort

Ilio Mare

A large, long established beachfront resort near Skala Prinos on the east coast, close to Golden Beach. Outdoor and children's pools, a poolside bar, two restaurants, a fitness centre and an outdoor tennis court, right on the sand. A straightforward do everything on site option for families. I can price this one up for you.

Best for spa & wellness

Alexandra Beach Thassos Spa Resort

Beachside resort in Potos on the south coast, with two outdoor pools, a full spa offering massages and facials, a sauna and an outdoor tennis court. Two restaurants and several bars round it out. A good pick for clients who want a slower, more spa focused week. I can price this one up for you.

Facilities as published at the time of writing. I always check current pricing, board basis and availability before booking.

Getting around

A hire car is genuinely the best way to see Thassos, the ring road around the island is only around 100km and lets you reach the marble beaches, mountain villages and the old capital Theologos easily in a day. Local buses (the KTEL network) run between the main towns and are a cheap, workable option if you're staying put in one resort and just want the odd day out. Taxis are easy to find in Limenas and the bigger resorts but cost more for longer journeys around the island. Most hotels are a short walk from their nearest beach, and Limenas itself is easily walkable for shops, restaurants and the ferry port.

Places to visit

Giola Lagoon

A natural rock pool carved into the coastline near Astris, filled with seawater and framed by rocks you can jump from. One of the most photographed spots on the island, best visited early or late in the day to avoid the crowds.

Theologos

The island's old capital, a mountain village of white marble and grey slate roofed houses a short walk from the twin waterfalls and ponds at Kefalogourna. A proper step away from the coast and resorts.

Saliara Marble Beach

A striking white marble beach near Thassos Town, the stone ground down into bright white pebbles and sand against turquoise water. Shade is limited, so it's worth bringing your own.

Ancient Agora & the Archaeological Museum, Limenas

The remains of the island's ancient marketplace sit right in the middle of modern Limenas, next to a small museum and the old harbour. A quick, easy way to fit a bit of history into a beach day.

Multi centre: combining Thassos with the Greek mainland

Because every trip to Thassos already passes through the mainland, it pairs naturally with a few days on the Greek mainland rather than another island. Here are three real ways I'd put it together.

Thassos & Kavala

The simplest extension, a few nights at the Imaret, a beautifully restored 19th century hotel in Kavala's historic Panagia old town, right where the ferries to Thassos depart. Easy to add a day trip to the Philippi archaeological site, a UNESCO World Heritage listed ancient city, along the way.

Thassos & Thessaloniki

Beach time on Thassos followed by a city break at the Electra Palace Thessaloniki, a 5 star hotel right on Aristotelous Square in Greece's second city, known for its food scene, waterfront promenade and Byzantine sites. Around a 2 hour road transfer from the Keramoti ferry port.

Thassos & Halkidiki

For clients who want two very different beach weeks, finish the trip at the Danai Beach Resort & Villas, a 5 star hotel in Nikiti on the Sithonia peninsula, around 1 hour 20 minutes by road from Thessaloniki. Pine forested coastline and quieter beaches than Thassos's busiest spots.

These are built as separate bookings combined into one trip rather than a single packaged product, message me and I'll put the whole itinerary and transfers together for you.
