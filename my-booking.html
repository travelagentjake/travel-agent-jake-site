Direct debit, the important bits

  • 1Your booking needs to be eligible. Your travel date needs to be at least 18 weeks away for a direct debit to be an option.
  • 2Your first payment needs a bit of notice. Whichever date you choose, it needs to be at least 4 weeks from today so there's time to get everything set up.
  • 3Your final payment date is fixed. Monthly plans always finish 6 weeks before you travel, this is calculated automatically and can't be moved.
  • 4Nothing happens automatically from this page. This calculator gives you an estimate. Once you click "Set up now", I'll check the details and get in touch to confirm everything before your direct debit is actually created.
