ORLANDO, FLORIDA

Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando Resort and Kennedy Space Center all within reach of one base, plus real hotels, weather by month and what a proper Orlando holiday actually costs.

What Jake says about Orlando

Orlando is the one holiday that almost every family asks me about at some point, because there simply is not another destination that packs in this many theme parks, water parks and big days out within one short drive. I can put it together as a proper flight inclusive package, and for anyone who wants their flights and hotel booked together under ATOL protection, that is how I book it.

It is also a holiday that rewards a bit of planning. Which theme park group you stay closest to, how many park days you actually need, and when you travel all change the trip quite a lot, so I always talk clients through the real choices rather than just picking the first hotel that comes up.

!
Jake's top tip

I always ask how many theme park days a client actually wants before booking anything. Two weeks sounds like a lot until you add up Disney's four parks, Universal's two, a water park day and at least one rest day by the pool.

Trip length & who it suits

Flights take around 9 hours each way, so most of my clients do 10 to 14 nights to make the long flight worthwhile, and two weeks is genuinely common given how much there is to see. It suits families with children of any age, since Disney in particular is built around very young children as much as teenagers, multi generational groups travelling together, honeymooners who want a livelier trip than a beach week, and groups of friends after big rides and late nights. It is less suited to anyone wanting a purely relaxing holiday, since even the pool days tend to be at a busy resort hotel rather than a quiet retreat.

Getting there

Direct flights run from several UK airports, including Gatwick and Manchester, to Melbourne Orlando International Airport on Florida's Space Coast, which replaced the older Orlando Sanford Airport on this route a few years ago. It is about an hour's transfer from Melbourne to Walt Disney World, Universal Orlando Resort and International Drive, and the Space Coast location is genuinely handy if a client also wants to add on Kennedy Space Center. Flight time is around 9 hours outbound. British Airways and Virgin Atlantic also fly direct into Orlando International Airport itself, closer to the main resort area, for anyone who would rather book flights independently of a package.

!
Jake's top tip

All transfers between Melbourne Airport and your hotel are included in a package. Hiring a car instead is also very popular for this trip, since it gives total flexibility over which park you visit and when.

Florida's hurricane season officially runs from June through to November, with the highest risk in August, September and October. It is worth taking out proper travel insurance for any trip in this window and keeping an eye on the forecast close to departure, though direct hits on the Orlando area itself are far less common than on the coast.

!
Jake's top tip

The afternoon thunderstorms from June to September are usually short and predictable, often clearing within an hour, but they do mean outdoor rides pause for safety. I always suggest a late morning start and an evening finish in the parks during these months.

Best time to visit

January and February are generally the quietest and best value months, with mild, dry weather, though some rides do close for scheduled refurbishment in this slower period. Spring break, roughly mid March to mid April, brings a noticeable jump in crowds and prices. May is a good value shoulder month before the summer holidays hit. June to August is the busiest school holiday stretch, hot, humid and prone to afternoon storms, but also when most UK families are actually able to travel. September and October are quieter after the US Labor Day holiday, with Halloween Horror Nights at Universal and Mickey's Not So Scary Halloween Party at Disney running through this period, alongside the highest hurricane season risk. November and December bring out the Christmas decorations and are genuinely beautiful, but Thanksgiving week and the Christmas to New Year fortnight are among the busiest and most expensive of the entire year.

Where to stay

Orlando splits into a few distinct areas, and which one suits a client depends on which parks matter most to them and how much they want to spend.

Walt Disney World Resort

Staying on Disney property means free transport around all four Disney parks, Disney's own theming carried right through to the hotel, and early entry to the parks for on site guests. There are 31 named Walt Disney World hotels to choose from, ranging from the value All Star resorts right up to the flagship Grand Floridian.

Universal Orlando Resort

The right choice for anyone prioritising Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure. Every Universal on site hotel gets free transport to the parks and Early Park Admission, and there are several of Universal's own hotels to choose from, including the retro styled Cabana Bay Beach Resort.

International Drive & Kissimmee villa country

The best value base for bigger family groups, roughly 20 to 25 minutes from both Disney and Universal. There is a choice of named villa communities around Davenport, Haines City, Clermont and Kissimmee, several with their own private pools, which can work out cheaper per head than a hotel room for larger families.

!
Jake's top tip

Disney no longer runs its old free Magical Express airport shuttle, so I always check current transfer arrangements when booking a Disney hotel directly rather than assuming it is still included.

Getting around

Hiring a car is very popular for an Orlando trip and often the easiest way to move between theme park groups, remembering that Florida drives on the right. Disney and Universal each run their own free internal transport, buses, monorail and boats at Disney, and buses and water taxis at Universal, between their on site hotels and their own parks, so clients staying on property rarely need a car just for park days. International Drive also has its own free I-Ride Trolley running the length of the strip, which is handy for anyone based around I-Drive without a hire car.

Recommended hotels

One hotel across three budgets.

Value

Disney's All Star Movies Resort

Disney's own value tier, themed around classic Disney films, with the usual free transport to all four Disney parks and Disney's theming running right through the resort. The one I point budget conscious Disney first timers towards. I can price this one up for you.

Family / moderate

Universal's Cabana Bay Beach Resort

A large, retro 1950s and 60s styled hotel on Universal property, with its own lazy river and bowling alley, free transport to Universal's parks and Early Park Admission. A strong value pick for anyone prioritising Universal over Disney. Happy to price this one up for you.

Luxury

Disney's Grand Floridian Resort & Spa

Disney's flagship Victorian style resort on the Magic Kingdom monorail loop, with a private beach, spa and some of the best dining on Disney property. The one I book for honeymoons and special occasion trips. Happy to check availability for your dates.

Places to visit

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex

About an hour east of Orlando on the Space Coast, genuinely handy if flying into Melbourne. Rocket garden, the Atlantis shuttle exhibit and, with the right timing, a real rocket launch visible from the grounds.

Everglades airboat & wildlife tours

A different side of Florida entirely, with airboat rides through the wetlands and a good chance of spotting alligators. A popular half day add on, usually with transport from an Orlando hotel included.

ICON Park & International Drive

A huge entertainment strip with its own 400 foot observation wheel, mini golf, an aquarium and a long run of restaurants. A good evening out without needing a theme park ticket.

Lake Eola Park, downtown Orlando

A world away from the theme parks, this lakeside park in downtown Orlando has swan shaped paddle boats, a farmers market on Sundays and a proper city skyline view. Worth a half day for anyone wanting a break from the parks.
