FINNISH LAPLAND

Husky and reindeer sleigh rides, a meeting with Santa Claus, and a genuine shot at the Northern Lights above the Arctic Circle, three real TUI resorts compared, weather by month and what it actually costs.

What Jake says about Finnish Lapland

Lapland is one of the few holidays I sell where the cold is the whole point. TUI is currently the only one of my three operators, TUI, Jet2holidays and easyJet holidays, that packages it as a proper holiday, flights, transfers, accommodation and a taster of the classic activities all bundled together. Jet2 does fly seasonal charters into several Lapland airports and easyJet has recently added its own routes too, but at the time of writing neither Jet2holidays nor easyJet holidays has turned those flights into an actual package holiday, so TUI is the one I book this through.

It is also a different shaped trip to almost everything else in this guide. Instead of a week by a pool, you are looking at a short, high impact break built around meeting Santa, a husky or reindeer sleigh ride, and a genuine chance of seeing the aurora overhead.

!
Jake's top tip

I set expectations early with clients on this one. It is a short, cold, activity packed break rather than a long relaxing one, and thermal suits and boots are provided as part of the package because you will need them.

Trip length & who it suits

TUI's core Lapland product is a short break, typically three or four nights, with flights, return transfers, half board accommodation, thermal clothing and a taster session of sleigh and husky rides all included as standard, plus a visit to Santa and a festive meal. Longer stays, extra activities such as snowmobiling (minimum age 12), extra husky or reindeer time, and dedicated Northern Lights tours can all be added on top. It suits families with young children especially well, since the Santa visit is built into every trip, but equally suits couples and small groups after a short, dramatic break chasing the aurora rather than a long holiday. Levi also works for anyone wanting proper downhill skiing or snowboarding alongside the classic Lapland activities, and tends to suit longer stays than a three night Santa trip.

Getting there

Flights take around 3 hours 30 minutes from the UK. TUI flies from a range of regional airports including Gatwick, Manchester, Newcastle, Glasgow, Birmingham and Belfast, landing at Rovaniemi, Kittila (for Levi) or Ivalo (for Saariselka) depending on which resort you book. British Airways also now runs its own direct Gatwick to Rovaniemi service through the winter season, for anyone wanting to book flights independently of a package.

!
Jake's top tip

All transfers between the airport and your hotel are included in a TUI package, and your full itinerary, including which day each activity happens, is confirmed through the TUI Santa Trips app a couple of days before you travel.

Weather by month

Lapland sits either on or above the Arctic Circle, so winters are long, dark and properly cold, and summers bring near constant daylight instead.

The Northern Lights can appear any time the sky is dark enough, roughly September through to March, with the long, properly dark nights of December to February generally rated the best odds, weather and solar activity allowing. There is no guarantee on any single trip, since it comes down to clear skies and the aurora actually being active that night.

!
Jake's top tip

June and July flip the whole picture, with the midnight sun above the Arctic Circle meaning it never really gets dark. It is a completely different trip to the snowy winter one, worth knowing if a client pictures Lapland as snow all year round.

Best time to visit

December is the classic choice for the full snowy Christmas look and the best odds of a white landscape for a Santa visit, though it is also the busiest and most expensive month by some distance. January to March tends to offer better value, deeper snow cover and longer, darker nights for Northern Lights hunting, at the cost of some seriously low temperatures. November is a quieter, cheaper shoulder month, though snow cover can be hit or miss that early in the season.

Where to stay

TUI sells Lapland across three main resort areas, each with its own character and its own named hotels, and all bookable through TUI.

Rovaniemi

Santa Claus's official hometown, right on the Arctic Circle line itself, and the easiest base for a classic short break built around Santa Claus Village, husky and reindeer sleigh rides and a central city with restaurants and shops. Named hotels include Hotel Santa Claus, Arctic City Hotel, Santasport Hotel and the Christmas Cottages.

Saariselka

Finland's northernmost resort, set among the fells of Urho Kekkonen National Park, further above the Arctic Circle and generally quieter and darker, which makes it a strong choice for Northern Lights hunting. Named hotels include Santa's Hotel Tunturi, Holiday Club Saariselka and Hotel Snowflake and Apartments.

Levi

Lapland's biggest ski resort, with proper downhill pistes alongside the usual husky, reindeer and Santa activities, so the one I point skiers and snowboarders towards. Named hotels include Hotel K5 Levi, Levi Panorama, Crazy Reindeer and the Lapland Hotel Sirkantahti.

!
Jake's top tip

Facilities, board basis and exact inclusions vary by hotel and by resort, so I always confirm the live details for a specific hotel before a client books.

Getting around

Return airport transfers are included in every TUI package, and your included activities, such as the husky or reindeer sleigh taster, are collected from and returned to your hotel as part of the trip. You do not need to arrange anything yourself once you land. Anyone wanting to explore further independently, or combine more than one resort in a single trip, can also self drive, since Finland drives on the right, the opposite side to the UK.

Things to do

A husky ride, a reindeer sleigh ride and a visit to Santa are included as standard on a TUI break, but most clients add at least one extra activity once they are there. These are some of the best rated options around Rovaniemi.

Three ways to see Lapland with TUI

All three of TUI's main resorts are genuine, bookable holidays rather than a generic add on, and each suits a slightly different client.

Rovaniemi, the classic Santa break

A three or four night short break built around Santa Claus Village, crossing the Arctic Circle line itself, and the included husky and reindeer sleigh taster. The easiest, most central option, and the one I book most often for families with young children.

Saariselka, the wilderness and aurora break

A quieter, darker base further north among the fells of Urho Kekkonen National Park, with the same included activities plus cross country skiing and snowshoeing on the doorstep. The one I book when Northern Lights viewing is the main priority.

Levi, the ski and Santa break

Lapland's biggest ski resort, for clients who want proper downhill skiing or snowboarding built around the same core Lapland activities, and generally the resort I steer longer stays and keen skiers towards.

TUI's own "from" pricing for a three to four night Lapland break generally starts from around £900 to £1,350 per adult at time of writing, covering flights, transfers, accommodation, half board and the included activity taster, and moves around a great deal with resort, hotel, dates and departure airport.

!
Jake's top tip

TUI's Design Your Trip tool also sells a genuine self drive, land only multi centre tour combining Rovaniemi and Saariselka in one trip for anyone who wants to see both. It does not include flights, so I book the flights separately alongside it, just ask me for current pricing.

Places to visit

Santa Claus Village & the Arctic Circle line

A purpose built village right on the Arctic Circle line itself, just outside Rovaniemi, where you can meet Santa in his grotto year round and get a certificate for crossing into the Arctic Circle. Included as standard on a TUI Rovaniemi break.

Ranua Wildlife Park

An Arctic zoo around an hour south of Rovaniemi, home to polar bears, brown bears, wolverines and lynx in large outdoor enclosures. A popular full day add on for families staying in or around Rovaniemi.

Urho Kekkonen National Park

A vast wilderness national park right on Saariselka's doorstep, criss crossed with cross country skiing and snowshoeing trails through the fells. One of the best spots in Finland for a dark sky and genuine aurora hunting.

Levi fell

Lapland's largest ski area, with pistes for every ability plus a gondola up to the summit for Northern Lights viewing or simply the view, even for clients who are not skiing themselves.
