AUSTRALIA: SYDNEY & BEYOND

The Opera House, the Harbour Bridge, golden beaches minutes from the city centre and some of the friendliest people you will meet anywhere. Australia is a long way from the UK, but for most of my clients it is the trip of a lifetime. Here is exactly how I put together a trip built around Sydney, the hotels I can price up for you, and what it actually costs.

What Jake says about Australia

Australia is such a huge country that I always start by narrowing it down, and for most first time visitors from the UK that means Sydney. It has the harbour, the Opera House, the bridge, beaches like Bondi and Manly right on the doorstep and enough in and around the city to fill a week on its own. Because of how far it is to fly, I rarely sell Sydney in isolation though. I put together flights, transfers and named hotels across the suppliers I work with to build a trip around you, whether that is Sydney alone or a proper multi centre taking in the Great Barrier Reef, Melbourne or the Red Centre as well.

I compare prices and routing across the suppliers I work with to find the best fit for your dates and budget.

Jake's top tip

Australia is genuinely a once in a lifetime trip for most of my clients given the flight time involved, so I always build in enough time on the ground to make the journey worth it. A week is the bare minimum for Sydney alone.

Trip length & who it suits

Sydney alone is worth five to seven nights, long enough for the harbour, the Blue Mountains, a few of the beaches and some proper downtime. Given how far Australia is to fly, most of my clients build a longer trip around it, two to three weeks in total, combining Sydney with Cairns and the Great Barrier Reef, Melbourne or Uluru, or continuing on to explore more of the country independently.

It suits first time visitors, couples, honeymooners, families over the UK's summer school holidays, and anyone who loves a mix of city, beach and bucket list wildlife or scenery. It is less suited to clients wanting a short break, since the flight time alone takes a day out of the trip each way, those on a tight budget, since Australia is one of the pricier long haul destinations I book, and anyone who cannot commit to the long flight.

Getting there

There are no direct flights between the UK and Australia, so every route involves at least one stopover, commonly Singapore, Dubai or Doha. Total journey time to Sydney typically runs from around 22 to 23 hours including the stopover, depending on your route and connection time. Airlines flying the route include Emirates, Qatar Airways, Singapore Airlines, Etihad, Cathay Pacific and Qantas with British Airways, usually connecting via the Middle East or Asia.

Sydney Airport sits around a thirty minute train or taxi ride from the city centre, so for most clients this is a straightforward arrival, whether Sydney is the whole trip or the start of a wider multi centre.

!
Jake's top tip

I always build in a proper rest day at the start of any Australia trip. Crossing that many time zones catches people out, and clients who push straight into sightseeing on day one almost always regret it.

Jake's top tip

Sydney summer brings real humidity alongside the heat, and January and February are also peak bushfire season in parts of the wider region, worth knowing for anyone planning to drive further afield. It rarely affects the city itself, but I always mention it honestly.

Best time to visit

December to February is Sydney's summer and a brilliant time to visit, hot, lively and home to the famous New Year's Eve fireworks over the harbour, but it is also the busiest and most expensive period, with warm humid days. I often point clients towards the shoulder seasons instead, March to May for settled weather and fewer crowds, or September to November for spring warmth before the summer rush, both with noticeably better value than peak summer. June to August is Sydney's mild winter, a quieter time to visit and a good season for whale watching along the coast.

!
Jake's top tip

If a client's trip includes the Great Barrier Reef, I always flag that the wet season runs roughly November to April in Far North Queensland, bringing heat, humidity and the chance of tropical downpours. June to October is the more reliable window for diving and snorkelling.

Where to stay in Sydney

Four real, bookable picks around the city.

Best value

Ibis Sydney World Square

A straightforward, well located hotel in the heart of the CBD, an easy walk to Darling Harbour, Chinatown and the main shopping streets. A sensible, comfortable base for a first trip. I can price this one up for you.

Best boutique & design

Harbour Rocks by Ode Hotels

A beautifully restored heritage building in The Rocks, Sydney's oldest quarter, just a short stroll from Circular Quay, the Opera House and the bridge. A lovely choice for couples who want history and character. I can price this one up for you.

Best family & resort feel

Four Points by Sheraton Sydney, Darling Harbour

Right on Darling Harbour with a well known rooftop infinity pool looking out over the water, close to the aquarium, the museums and plenty of family friendly restaurants. A great fit for families. I can price this one up for you.

Best luxury

Park Hyatt Sydney

Right on the harbour's edge beside the Opera House with some of the best views in the city, elegant rooms, a rooftop pool and a destination restaurant. A real treat for honeymooners or a special occasion. I can price this one up for you.

Facilities as published at the time of writing. I always check current pricing, board basis and availability before booking.

Getting around

Central Sydney is easy to get around on foot, with Circular Quay, The Rocks, the Opera House and Darling Harbour all within walking distance of each other. Beyond that, Sydney has a good network of trains, buses, light rail and ferries, all covered by a single Opal card that you tap on and off, and the ferry across to Manly or Taronga Zoo is one of the best value harbour views in the city. For the Blue Mountains, a train from Central Station or a day tour both work well without needing a hire car. Taxis and ride share are plentiful and reasonably priced for shorter hops, especially late at night.

Places to visit

Sydney Opera House

One of the most recognisable buildings on earth, worth seeing up close on a guided tour as well as from the water, with a full programme of opera, theatre and live music through the year.

Sydney Harbour Bridge

Walk across it for free, or book the full summit climb for panoramic views over the harbour, the Opera House and the whole city skyline. Either way it is a Sydney icon worth making time for.

The Blue Mountains

A UNESCO listed national park around ninety minutes from the city, home to the Three Sisters rock formation, eucalyptus forest that gives the mountains their blue haze, and some genuinely spectacular lookouts and walking trails.

Taronga Zoo & Bondi Beach

Taronga Zoo sits right on the harbour with some of the best skyline views in Sydney alongside koalas and kangaroos, while Bondi Beach and its famous coastal walk to Coogee are an easy bus or ferry ride from the city centre.

Multi centre: combining Sydney with the rest of Australia

Given how far Australia is to fly, I almost always encourage clients to see more than just Sydney. Here are three real ways I would put it together.

Sydney & Cairns

For the Great Barrier Reef and the Daintree Rainforest, finish the trip at the Pullman Reef Hotel Casino on the Cairns Esplanade, a short transfer from the marina where most reef cruises depart.

Sydney & Melbourne

For laneway cafes, world class food and sport, add on a few nights at the Grand Hyatt Melbourne on Collins Street, right in the heart of the city and close to the Yarra River.

Sydney & Uluru

For clients who want the Red Centre as well as the coast, add on a stay at Sails in the Desert, part of Ayers Rock Resort, with uninterrupted views of Uluru and Kata Tjuta on the doorstep.

These are built as separate bookings combined into one trip rather than a single packaged product, message me and I will put the whole itinerary and transfers together for you.
