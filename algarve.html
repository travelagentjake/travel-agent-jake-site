THE ALGARVE

Weather by month, where to stay, things to do and what it actually costs, everything you need to plan a trip to Portugal's most popular holiday coast.

What Jake says about the Algarve

The Algarve is one of the easiest sells in my job. It is an hour's flight shorter than most of the Mediterranean, flights run there in huge numbers, and it genuinely suits almost every type of client, from a family wanting a big all inclusive resort to a couple of golfers wanting four rounds in a week to grandparents wanting somewhere warm and easy for a week in October.

What makes it stand out from Spain's other coasts is the sheer range packed into one region. You have the lively strip of Albufeira, the marina and golf courses of Vilamoura, the dramatic cliffs and beaches around Lagos, and the quiet, exclusive golf resorts of Quinta do Lago and Vale do Lobo, all within about an hour of Faro Airport.

!
Jake's top tip

The Algarve is so well served by flights that price and hotel choice usually matter more than flight availability when I am quoting it. I always ask a client how they want to spend their days, on the beach, on the golf course, or exploring, before picking the area.

Trip length & who it suits

Seven nights is by far the most popular length, long enough to properly relax and fit in a boat trip or a round of golf without feeling rushed. Short breaks of three or four nights work well for a quick golf trip or a city style break around Faro and Tavira, while two weeks suits clients who want real value out of the flight, particularly in the quieter months.

It suits families after a big resort with pools and kids clubs, couples wanting an easy, reliable week in the sun, golf groups chasing some of Europe's best courses, and older clients who want warm, mild weather without a long flight. It is less suited to a client who wants a totally unspoilt, undeveloped coastline, since the central Algarve is well and truly on the tourist map.

Getting there

Faro Airport (FAO) is the only airport serving the Algarve, and every resort area, Albufeira, Vilamoura, Lagos and Quinta do Lago, is within about an hour's transfer. Flights run to Faro from a huge range of UK airports, including Birmingham, Bristol, East Midlands, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Leeds Bradford, London Gatwick, London Heathrow, London Luton, London Stansted, Manchester and Newcastle, so availability is rarely an issue.

Flight time is around 2 hours 40 minutes from London and the south of England, a little longer from Scotland and Northern Ireland.

!
Jake's top tip

Because so many flights run here, the Algarve is one of the easier destinations to find a flight time that suits a client, even for a short midweek break. It is worth checking which airport your client is closest to though, since not every regional airport runs every week of the year.

Best time to visit

April to June and September to October are the sweet spot, warm and sunny without the full force of midsummer heat, and generally better value than peak school holiday weeks. July and August are the hottest months, often touching the high twenties, and also the busiest and most expensive. November to March is the Algarve's mild winter season, popular with golfers and clients chasing guaranteed sunshine at lower prices, though evenings do turn cool.

Where to stay

The Algarve's central and western resort areas each have a distinctly different feel, all reachable from Faro Airport within about an hour.

Albufeira & the Central Strip

The Algarve's biggest and liveliest resort town, with the Strip's bars and clubs alongside a traditional old town and some of the region's best beaches, including Praia da Falesia. By far the widest choice of hotels, from budget friendly to five star, and the easiest sell for a first time client.

Vilamoura & the Marina Coast

A purpose built resort centred on a large marina lined with restaurants and bars, surrounded by some of the Algarve's best known golf courses. Quieter and more upmarket than Albufeira, with a good mix of hotels, apartments and villas, and a strong pull for golfers and couples.

Lagos & the West

A historic harbour town with real Portuguese character, surrounded by the Algarve's most dramatic coastline, including the cliffs and sea caves of Ponta da Piedade. Smaller and more low rise than the central resorts, popular with couples and clients who want beauty and atmosphere alongside the beach.

Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo & the Golf Coast

The Algarve's most exclusive corner, built around championship golf courses, the Ria Formosa lagoon and some genuinely stunning beaches. Home to the region's top five star hotels and private villas. The quietest and priciest of the four areas, and a strong fit for a luxury or golf focused client.

!
Jake's top tip

For a first timer who wants the biggest hotel choice and some nightlife on the doorstep, Albufeira is the easier sell. For a golfer or a client who has done Spain before and wants something quieter and more polished, Vilamoura or the Quinta do Lago area is the one to steer them towards.

Getting around

Airport transfers are normally included as part of the package. Because the resort areas are fairly spread out, car hire is popular and gives the most freedom to explore, especially for clients wanting to combine a couple of areas or visit Silves, Faro or Tavira under their own steam. Taxis and ride hailing apps are widely available and reasonably priced for shorter trips, and most hotels also offer local shuttle services or can arrange transfers to nearby towns and golf courses.

Recommended hotels

Five real, bookable picks spread across the main resort areas.

Best value

Clube Praia da Oura, Albufeira

A straightforward, good value apartment style hotel a short walk from Praia da Oura beach and the Strip, with a large pool, sun terrace and easy access to Albufeira's restaurants and nightlife. A solid budget friendly pick for families and groups.

Best for families

Vila Gale Marina, Vilamoura

A large, family friendly hotel close to Vilamoura's marina, with multiple pools, a kids' club and easy access to the restaurants and bars around the water. A strong choice for families wanting plenty of facilities on site without being right in the centre of Albufeira.

Best adults only

Vila Maria Hotel Adult Only, Praia da Luz, Lagos

A boutique adults only hotel in the quiet coastal village of Praia da Luz, near Lagos, with a relaxed pool terrace and easy access to the beach and the cliffs of Ponta da Piedade. A good fit for couples wanting a quieter base away from the busier central resorts.

Best for design & style

W Algarve, Almancil

A stylish, design led beachfront hotel near Almancil, on the edge of the Quinta do Lago golf coast, with striking architecture, a spa and a lively pool scene. Suits couples and groups wanting a more fashion forward base than the traditional resort hotels.

Best for golf & luxury

Conrad Algarve, Quinta do Lago

A five star resort set in the Quinta do Lago golf estate, with spacious grounds, multiple restaurants, a spa and direct access to some of Europe's top courses. The standout luxury pick for golfers and couples wanting a genuinely five star base.

Hotel availability, board basis and pricing change regularly, always confirm the live details with Jake before booking.

Places to visit

A few of the sights worth building into an itinerary beyond the hotel pool.

Benagil Cave

A huge sea cave with an open skylight roof and its own small beach inside, reachable only by boat, kayak or paddleboard. One of the most photographed spots on the whole Algarve coast, and usually booked as a short boat trip from Albufeira, Armacao de Pera or Lagos.

Albufeira Old Town

The whitewashed, cobbled heart of Albufeira, with narrow lanes, local restaurants and a small beach tucked beneath the cliffs, a calmer contrast to the Strip's bars just up the hill. An easy half day out even for clients based elsewhere in the resort.

Silves Castle

A red sandstone Moorish castle overlooking the town of Silves and the Arade river, once the Algarve's medieval capital. Climbing the walls gives sweeping views over the surrounding orange groves, and the town below has a good choice of cafes for lunch.

Faro Old Town

The Algarve's capital, often overlooked by clients staying in the resorts, with a walled old town, a marina and the Ria Formosa lagoon right on its doorstep. Worth an afternoon on the way to or from the airport, or as a day trip in its own right.

Jake's top tip

Cards are accepted almost everywhere in the Algarve, including small cafes and taxis, so there is less need to carry large amounts of cash than in some other winter sun destinations.
